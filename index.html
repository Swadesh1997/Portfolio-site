01 / About

From building apps in the field to asking research questions about them

I studied Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University on a merit scholarship. I graduated in 2021 with High Distinction and a CGPA of 3.71. My undergraduate research on gene expression classification was published at IEEE ICMLC 2021.

At WaterAid Bangladesh I look after the apps and data systems that program teams rely on. They range from a menstrual health app with more than 45,000 registered users to a customer service platform for Chattogram WASA. That work showed me what gets in the way of data-driven decisions in low-resource settings: poor data quality, systems that do not connect, small teams and unequal access.

My research questions come from there. How can machine learning and spatial data help municipalities plan sanitation? How do we build health and service apps that people can trust and use? I want to study these questions properly, with a supervisor and a lab behind me.

04 / Projects

Systems I have built and managed

PRJ_001 | Digital health

Ananya menstrual health app

A Flutter mobile app, backed by a Django API, that gives women and girls in underserved communities menstrual health information. It has period tracking, hospital and helpline details and an AI chatbot built with program teams and medical reviewers. I built version 1.0 and now manage its development. More than 45,000 users have registered.

Flutter, Django, Google Play | WaterAid Bangladesh, 2023 to present

PRJ_002 | Public services

Chattogram WASA customer service platform

A bilingual platform for billing, online payments, new connection applications, complaints, notifications and service tracking. The rollout covers a mobile app, a web portal, USSD and IVR. I turn utility workflows into digital requirements and work with the developer team so managers can see where requests get stuck.

Chattogram WASA with WaterAid Bangladesh

PRJ_003 | Public services

Area-based water tariff platform

A web platform for area-based water tariffs. I designed the role-based access (viewer, editor and admin) that decides who can see, change or manage the data.

WaterAid Bangladesh

PRJ_004 | Geospatial

Municipal sanitation and WASH information systems

GIS-enabled workflows that bring spatial and administrative data together for sanitation planning. They include the Integrated Municipal Information System for Lalmonirhat Municipality and digital data collection for public toilets, water quality and service coverage.

Lalmonirhat and other municipalities

PRJ_005 | Geospatial

GIS drone survey and WASH assessment

I planned and coordinated a drone survey and WASH situation assessment in Lalmonirhat and Paikgacha with SNV. A specialist mapping vendor did the flying and processing, and I managed scope, timelines and the final report, delivered in May 2026.

WaterAid Bangladesh with SNV, 2026

PRJ_006 | Geospatial

Informal settlement detection from satellite imagery

I planned and led this project across the Dhaka North, Dhaka South and Narayanganj city corporations. An Enhanced U-Net model read 30 cm satellite imagery, and I designed a field check of 387 sites across 129 wards. The result shows the limits of using roofing material as a proxy for informal settlements. It is a working paper with WaterAid Bangladesh.

WaterAid Bangladesh, 2025, working paper
